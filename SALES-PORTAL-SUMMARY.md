# Ittehad Sales Portal: What Has Been Built

**Dealerships:** Hyundai Islamabad, Jetour Ittehad and CSM Ittehad
**Status:** Sales portal complete and tested from start (new customer) to finish (car delivered)

## What the portal does, in one line

It follows every customer from the first visit or phone call to the day they drive away in their new car, so everyone knows where each customer and each car stands.

## The journey of one customer

1. **Lead:** a customer visits or calls, and the salesperson enters their name, phone and the car they want.
2. **Follow-up and quotation:** the salesperson keeps notes of every call and prints a price quotation.
3. **Conversion:** the customer agrees to buy. The salesperson records the payment details, the customer's CNIC and the expected delivery month.
4. **Sales order:** the Sales Admin books the car, entering the PBO number from head office's software.
5. **Approval:** the Manager approves the order.
6. **Car on the way:** the car is marked *In transit*, then *Received* when it reaches the dealership.
7. **Delivery:** a date is fixed, the car is checked and the customer signs the delivery note.

Everyone involved gets a notification at each step.

## Salesperson

- Adds new customers (leads). If the phone number already belongs to another salesperson's customer, the portal says so and the case can be sent to the Assistant Manager.
- Records follow-up calls and visits.
- Prints **vehicle quotations** and **PPF vouchers**.
- Converts the customer when they agree to buy, entering the CNIC, the payment details and the expected delivery month or date.
- Sees a large, coloured **Car Status** box on each customer's page (Booked, In transit, Received, Ready for delivery, Delivered), so they can answer the customer straight away.
- Has a personal dashboard with their leads, conversions, cars booked and delivered, and reminders (e.g. customers not called for over a day).

## Assistant Manager

- Does everything a salesperson does, and can add leads for any salesperson.
- Handles **duplicate customers** sent by salespeople, and can convert them when the salesperson is away.
- Marks cars *In transit* and schedules deliveries.
- Adds and edits car models and variants, and changes the quotation and PPF formats.
- Sees the team's performance (**Track record**) and the dashboard by salesperson.

## Manager

- **Approves sales orders.**
- Sees the whole dealership: all leads, orders, deliveries and reports.
- **Manages staff:** creates logins for salespersons, the Assistant Manager, the Sales Admin and the Delivery Team.
- Adds models and variants, and changes document formats.
- Uses the **Delivery report**: cars delivered this month, this year, in the last few days or overall, per dealership or combined.

## Sales Admin

- Sees every converted customer waiting for a sales order.
- **Raises the sales order:** price, discount, booking amount and payment reference. The **PBO number is required**. The customer's CNIC is filled in already, and the Admin checks it against the CNIC copy and corrects it if needed.
- Enters the car's **chassis and engine numbers** once known.
- Marks cars *In transit* after approval and schedules deliveries.
- Can correct customer details after conversion.
- Searches orders by **PBO number, even by its last 2–3 digits**.

## Delivery Team (one team for all three dealerships)

- **Deliveries page:** every booked car in tabs: *Waiting for car · In transit · Received · Scheduled · Delivered*, with an **Overdue** filter.
- Is the only team that can mark a car **Received**.
- Manages **open stock**: registers incoming cars and links them to waiting orders.
- Completes the **pre-delivery checklist** (PDI done, documents ready, accessories fitted). The car cannot be handed over until all three are ticked.
- Prints the **delivery note** on each dealership's own letterhead, for the customer to sign.
- Gets alerts for deliveries due today and for cars that are late.

## Printed documents

| Document | Who prints it | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicle quotation | Salesperson, Assistant Manager | Each brand has its own format. Hyundai and Jetour can print on pre-printed letterhead paper. |
| PPF voucher | Salesperson, Assistant Manager | Filled in from the order (PBO, chassis, engine) |
| Delivery note | Delivery Team, Sales Admin | One page, on the dealership's own letterhead |

All documents can be previewed, downloaded as PDF or printed.

## Useful for everyone

- **Search** by customer name, phone, CNIC, chassis number or PBO number.
- **Notifications** (the bell icon) for each step that concerns you.
- **Action needed** list on every dashboard: what is waiting for you today.
- **Activity log:** who did what, and when.
- Works on computer and mobile, and every user sees only their own dealership's data.
